TappedIn

Make music.
Find your people.

A beatmaker finds clients in minutes. ML ranking finds musical fit, so the day goes back to making music instead of promotion.

The point

Spend less time promoting.

Searching for artists and sending cold messages eats the hours that should go to beats. TappedIn puts a ranked list of people whose sound fits in front of you.

Hear them first

One featured track, a music identity, and in-app playback. You know the fit before you write.

Then work together

Connect, leave feedback, chat, and send playable beats once both of you are in.

In the product

Music profile

Artist or producer, name, photo, location, experience, bio, and collaboration status.

Music identity

Genres, influences, type beats, moods, BPM range, and social links.

Featured audio

One track on the card, played in the app. The file uploads to S3 through a presigned URL.

Ranked feed

TF-IDF similarity and collaborative filtering. Like, skip, or save. Filter by role, genre, or location.

Connections

A mutual like connects you. Feedback on a track, plus notifications when that happens.

Chat and beats

Direct messages and playable beats after you connect, with read receipts. Email and phone sign-in.

Python 3.12 · FastAPI · Uvicorn · SQLAlchemy · asyncpg · Dishka · Alembic · Argon2 · boto3

Data

Production-style monorepo. Feature-Sliced Design on the client, DDD and Clean Architecture on the backend. Ranking is TF-IDF cosine similarity plus collaborative filtering. Sessions live in Redis.
